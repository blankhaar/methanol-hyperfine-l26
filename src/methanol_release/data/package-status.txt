L26 methanol hyperfine and Zeeman spectroscopy — version 0.1.0

Model: Lankhaar (2026), abbreviated L26.
Eigenfunctions: computed from bundled BELGI/GLOBALFIT source and Xu constants.
Products: fitted tensors, spectra, conditional uncertainty ranges, magnetic
catalogues, line-center shifts and candidate experimental transitions.

The numerical model, assumptions and validation limits are documented in docs/.
Conditional uncertainty ranges are not calibrated confidence intervals.
Project-authored software is EUPL-1.2. BELGI and other third-party components
retain their own terms and attribution.
Repository: https://github.com/blankhaar/methanol-hyperfine-l26
